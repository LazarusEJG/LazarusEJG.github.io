About Me

Greetings!

Hello! I'm EJ Gordon. I've watched anime for just about as long as I can remember. From my earlier days of watching classics like Code Geass in a box next to my parent's bed on Netflix, to now with the Crunchyroll subscription I pay for every month to watch the latest series. Over time, I branched out and started reading manga and then light novels too, which led me to make this website as part of my CSCE 242 Web Applications class at the University of South Carolina. I was inspired by probably the most prominent anime database website, MyAnimeList, and decided to make my own kind of database. I hope you enjoy having a look around the site.
